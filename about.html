About RoboCup

Understanding the Global Robotics Initiative

What is RoboCup?

RoboCup is an international robotics initiative that began in 1997 with the ambitious goal of developing autonomous robots capable of beating human soccer champions by 2050.

Since its inception, RoboCup has grown into one of the most significant robotics competitions in the world, encompassing both research and education. It serves as a platform for:

  • Developing cutting-edge robotics technology
  • Inspiring innovation and creativity
  • Fostering international collaboration
  • Promoting STEM education globally

Mission & Vision

Mission: To promote robotics and AI research and education through competitions and collaboration.

Vision: By 2050, a team of autonomous humanoid robots shall be able to win against the human world champion team in soccer.

RoboCup Junior (RCJ)

RoboCup Junior focuses on education and skill development for students up to 19 years of age.

Held in over 55 countries, RCJ is designed to inspire teamwork, innovation, and 21st-century STEM skills through three core competition categories.

Core Categories:

🚨 Rescue

Navigate through hazardous environments and rescue victims using autonomous robots.

⚽ Soccer

Program robots to play soccer autonomously, combining strategy and technical skills.

🎭 OnStage

Create an entertaining performance combining robotics, creativity, and storytelling.

Indian RoboCup Junior Foundation

Bringing the global robotics revolution to India

The Indian RoboCup Junior Foundation is dedicated to promoting robotics education and competition in India. We organize regional, national, and international competitions to identify and nurture talent among students across the country.

Our mission is to prepare India's best teams for the prestigious RoboCup International 2027 in Nuremberg, Germany.
